# Candidate walkthrough and interview practice
This is a guided exercise prepared with AI assistance, not proof that the candidate has completed it.

## 30-minute walkthrough
1. Read README and inspect three fictional accounts. Explain why a need hypothesis is not a verified buying signal.
2. Rewrite one email in your own words: a relevant observation, a discovery question and a short meeting request. Do not invent a breach or product outcome.
3. Role-play the two cases in discovery_and_handoff.md. Ask about Budget, Authority, Need and Timeline. Explain why case A needs further discovery.
4. Run `python crm.py list`, then log a fictional interaction using README instructions. Run `python crm.py report` and explain unique accounts versus activity counts.
5. Calculate reply and attendance rates using the fictional metrics. Explain that these are not actual performance results.
6. Explain one real Thrift It Up customer interaction: customer need, your recommendation, purchase outcome and logistics responsibility. Discuss the INR 5 lakh+ month with its time period and your contribution; do not disclose private customer data.

## Interview prompts
Why move from ECE to sales? Connect technical learning aptitude with your actual selling experience.
What is the difference between retail selling and an SDR role? Explain researching accounts, qualifying need and handing off relevant opportunities, rather than claiming they are identical.
Have you used Zoho CRM? State your actual experience. This Python simulation does not establish Zoho use.
What did AI help with? Explain the toolkit was AI-assisted and describe what you independently reviewed or changed, only after doing so.
