# Simulated outreach and discovery
All account scenarios below are invented; verify a real trigger before using the message. Avoid unsupported breach or product-performance claims.

## Opening emails
Finance - Subject: Employee awareness during team growth
Hi [Name], in this sample scenario your payments team is growing. How are new employees trained to recognize phishing? If reviewing awareness is a current priority, would a 15-minute discovery call be useful?

Healthcare - Subject: Security onboarding across clinics
Hi [Name], with multiple clinics in this fictional example, how do you keep staff security onboarding consistent? I would like to understand the current process before discussing whether an employee-awareness solution might help. Is a short call relevant?

E-commerce - Subject: Awareness for seasonal staff
Hi [Name], this practice account is hiring seasonal staff. How do temporary employees learn to identify suspicious messages? If there is a gap to address, could we discuss it briefly next week?

Manufacturing - Subject: Training consistency across sites
Hi [Name], how do you deliver and measure security awareness across plant teams? I am exploring whether there is a need for a more structured approach. Would a short discovery conversation be useful?

Telecom - Subject: Employee phishing reporting
Hi [Name], how does your distributed support team report suspicious emails today? If improving that process is on your roadmap, would you be open to a 15-minute conversation?

## Follow-up sequence
Day 3: Is employee security awareness something you own, or is another colleague better placed to discuss it?
Day 7: Would reviewing your current training completion and reporting process be useful?
Day 12: I will close this thread for now. If this becomes a priority, I would be glad to reconnect. Please let me know if you prefer no further outreach.

## Cold-call and BANT discovery
Ask permission: Is now a reasonable time for a 30-second introduction?
Context: I am calling to understand your employee security awareness and suspicious-email reporting process.
Need: What works well today? What gap would you like to address, and what is its impact?
Authority: Who evaluates a change, and who approves it?
Budget: Has funding been discussed? If not, what is the planning process?
Timeline: Is there a target date or upcoming review?
Close: Agree on a useful next step, participants and agenda. Record unanswered questions as unknown.

## Objections
Existing provider: Is the current approach meeting your goals? If yes, avoid pressing for a meeting.
No budget: Ask whether a future planning cycle is relevant; do not invent qualification.
Send details: Ask which topic would be useful and whether a follow-up is welcome.
Not interested: Thank the prospect, record the preference and end the call.
